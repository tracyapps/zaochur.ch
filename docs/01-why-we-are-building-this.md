# Why We Are Building This

> **Status:** Draft v0.1 — for community review

## A word that means *to live*

Zao (ζάω) is a Greek word from the Bible. It means *"to live, breathe, be among the living (not lifeless, not dead)"* — even *"to have true life worthy of the name."*

It's the word Jesus used when he said our God is a God of the living, not the dead. It's how the Bible writers describe Jesus after the resurrection. We believe it is the call of all who seek after God — **to become fully alive**, here and now, not someday.

Any tool we gather in must serve that call: to make the people of this community *more* alive, more connected, more rested, more free. By that measure, the tool we gather in now is failing us.

## Our three pillars

Everything in these planning documents stands on the pillars already printed on our website. They are worth saying again here, because they — not convenience, not cost, not convention — drive every decision in this project.

### Jesus rooted
We root our community in the life, person, and teaching of Jesus Christ: a radical brown Palestinian peasant, the God who is with us, preaching love, justice, and resistance.

### Justice centered
The Gospel leads us directly into the work of justice — the active and engaged struggle for all to be free, both spiritually and physically. We align with Jesus, who always aligns with the oppressed and marginalized, against the powers and principalities of injustice.

### Radically inclusive
We seek to build a radically inclusive community and world, wherein all people are valued in the fullness of who God has made them to be — affirmed, celebrated, and loved.

Our full Statement of Beliefs lives on the Zao website. Two of its lines matter especially for this project:

> *"We believe the Good News of Jesus shows us the abundance of God's provision through communalism in contrast to the messages of scarcity and hoarding found in capitalism."*

> *"We believe the Gospel calls all believers to be in solidarity with those whose identities and social locations place them at the margins."*

## The house we are renting

Our Digital Commons — the online equivalent of our church building, designed to be both sacred and accessible — currently lives on Discord. Discord has served us: it is where we've laughed, grieved, worshipped, organized, and kept an 89-day Wordle streak alive. We honor that.

But it is a rented house, and the rent is higher than it looks:

- **We are vulnerable people in someone else's database.** Our community includes the most targeted people in this country right now — and our pastors have already had to leave it for their family's safety. Everything said in the Commons is stored, scanned, and monetized by a corporation with no covenant with us. We trust big tech to choose ethics over money, power, and government pressure exactly when it matters most — never.
- **It drains the very people it should shelter.** Many of us are disabled, chronically ill, mentally struggling, or simply exhausted from fighting systems built against us every single day. After hours spent tuning notifications, the app still hurts: an unbounded stream of unread, a guilt-shaped "catch up" pile, the sense that closing it means missing something. **We want to *want* to open our Commons. Not feel it draining us.**
- **The door is too narrow.** To enter, you need a Discord account, Discord knowledge, and Discord trust. Some beloved folks have all three missing. The front door of our church should not belong to a gaming company.
- **We cannot shape the space.** We reorganized our Discord into rooms of the church — the Sanctuary, the Living Room, the Library — because place matters. But we are arranging furniture in a rented hall, and we can't move the walls.

## The house we are called to build

So we are building the Zao Commons: a Digital Commons that belongs to the community, shaped like the church herself.

- **It looks like the building.** A calm, two-dimensional map of the church — the Front Door, the Sanctuary, the Living Room, the Library, the Rec Room, the Music Studio, the Art Gallery, the Neighborhood — with rooms for our Harbors and Affinity Groups. Not a 3D world (motion sickness, confusing interfaces, exclusion), but a *place*: you can see who's here, where they're gathered, and which room is warm.
- **It is calm by default.** No unread badges screaming. No infinite scroll. Quiet hours that are actually quiet. A gentle daily digest instead of a firehose. An app whose promise is: *open me, be with your people, close me, be at peace.*
- **It keeps its people safe.** Pseudonyms welcomed. Minimal data kept. Servers in jurisdictions that protect rather than pursue us. Safety practices written down, in the open, before a line of code.
- **It carries our whole life together.** Worship and prayer in the Sanctuary. The Commons Bank — our time bank, where an hour of anyone's time equals an hour of anyone's time — built in from the start. Body doubling rooms. An art gallery for our work. The daily games ritual we already love. Ways to find a companion for a march or a ride to a harbor.
- **The door is wide.** A link and a welcome. No Discord account, no technical knowledge, no trust in big tech required. From viewer to true participant — the promise of our own Join page, finally kept by a place that can keep it.

## What this is not

- **It is not a startup.** Nothing here will ever be sold, monetized, optimized for engagement, or grown for an exit. We build at the speed of trust and rest; burnout is a bug, not a badge.
- **It is not a wall.** The Commons will bridge to Discord during a long, patient migration, because leaving people behind is not inclusion.
- **It is not finished.** These documents are drafts, offered for the community's discernment. What we build will be shaped by the people it must shelter — especially those the current tools hurt most.

## Why now

Because the need is now. Because we already have the muscle: the Commons Bank exists, the rooms are named, the community is gathered, and the daily rituals (a Wordle grid shared among friends is not nothing — it is communion, lightly disguised) are already ours. All that's missing is a house built to hold them.

Zao means *to live*. Let's build a place worthy of the name.
