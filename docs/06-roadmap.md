# Roadmap — Building the Commons

> **Status:** Draft v0.1 — for community review. **This page is written to live in the open at zaochur.ch.**
> **Pace:** We build at the speed of trust and rest. Phases, not promises — dates are decided with the community's actual capacity, never imposed on it. Burnout is a bug, not a badge.

Each phase ends at a **gate** — a question the community answers together before we proceed. If a gate says "not yet," that's the system working.

**Status legend:** ○ Not started · ◐ In progress · ● Landed

---

## Phase 0 — Listen & Foundations ◐ *(we are here)*

**Goal:** Decide together, on paper, what we're building and why — before a line of code.

- Draft the planning documents (this set) and open them for community comment
- **Listening conversations** — especially with members the current tools hurt most: disabled, chronically ill, overwhelmed, non-technical, privacy-fearing
- Discern and confirm the [Non-Negotiables](02-non-negotiables.md) and [Threat Model](03-safety-and-threat-model.md) with the community and pastors
- Form working groups: **Care** (safety & moderation), **Design** (brand, floor plan, calm), **Build** (technical), **Welcome** (migration & onboarding)
- Decide the name and home of the Commons together; publish the roadmap at zaochur.ch
- Confirm hosting and stack decisions (see [Technology](07-technology.md)) through the Care + Build groups
- Gather design assets and brand language into `branding-design/` (outside the repo — large files by design; finished assets are copied in deliberately)

**Gate 0:** *Do the non-negotiables feel like ours? Does the community say "yes, build it — this way"?*

---

## Phase 1 — The Front Door ○

**Goal:** A warm, useful, safe doorstep — useful immediately, and threatening to no one (including Discord, which stays fully alive).

- The join flow: link, welcome, the three pillars, a tour, chosen names & avatars
- Member profiles: pseudonym-friendly, self-authored tags, visibility controls
- **Events:** the community calendar — the first thing to move house, because time-boxed things move easiest
- **The Commons Bank ports home** (profiles, jobs, exchanges, appreciations, the safety pattern)
- The **daily digest** (email + in-app): today at Zao, gentle, two minutes
- Give-away shelf v1 in the Neighborhood

**Gate 1:** *Are people arriving through the front door without help, and does the weekly rhythm (events, bank, digest) run happily here?*

---

## Phase 2 — The Commons ○

**Goal:** The rooms open, the community conversation moves home — gently, one room at a time.

- The floor plan: the map of the church, rooms with doors, presence without surveillance
- **The Living Room, Library, and Neighborhood conversations** (chat done calmly: threads that breathe, no infinite scroll, "you're caught up")
- **The daily games ritual:** the community puzzle, spoiler-safe grids, the collective streak, the crowned daily digest; *doku port begins
- Affinity Spaces with real walls; Harbor hearths; the Sanctuary's prayer wall with care controls
- **Discord bridge:** messages flow both ways during migration, so nobody chooses between friends and tools
- DMs, export & delete, quiet-hours defaults all live

**Gate 2:** *Is daily life — chatter, prayer, play — happening here rather than on Discord? Is anyone stranded? (Stranded people are a gate-blocker, not a footnote.)*

---

## Phase 3 — Togetherness ○

**Goal:** Hear and see each other: gathered worship, quiet company, body doubling.

- Voice & video rooms, embedded from trusted open-source engines (LiveKit/Jitsi — we borrow, we don't build): the Sanctuary for Virtual Harbors, the Living Room for hangs
- **The Quiet Room:** body doubling, the shared candle, grief space
- Watch parties v1: synchronized watching side-by-side (each household's own streaming login — like inviting friends over to watch *your* TV)
- Events get "going together" superpowers: companions, rides, meet-at-the-door

**Gate 3:** *Can a full Virtual Harbor worship service run entirely in the Commons, warmly, for the least-tech-comfortable member?*

---

## Phase 4 — Play & Create ○

**Goal:** The joyful rooms — the reason to *want* to open the app.

- **draw-tionary comes home:** async drawing rounds + live guessing parties, spoiler-safe share cards
- The **Art Gallery:** walls, viewings, artist statements, collaborative works
- The **Music Studio:** layer-recording (the virtual round), song shares, listening parties
- More Rec Room games; community-made games; game nights on the calendar

**Gate 4:** *Is there art on the walls and music in the rooms? Are we playing weekly?*

---

## After the phases — Tend ○ (ongoing, forever)

The building is never "done," and neither is a church. Seasonal care: accessibility audits, privacy reviews, safety rehearsals, replenishing caretakers, retiring what we don't need. The roadmap then belongs to the community's next calling.

---

## Migration: how we cross the street without losing anyone

1. **Events first** (Phase 1) — time-boxed, low-risk, immediately useful
2. **One room at a time** (Phase 2) — each Discord room's stewards choose their moving week; the bridge keeps both sides talking during each move
3. **The games ritual moves early in Phase 2** — it's daily, beloved, and its momentum belongs to the new home
4. **Discord stays open as long as anyone still needs it** (INCLUSION-4). The bridge stays up until the last willing person is across. No cliff, no deadline, no guilt
5. **Archive with dignity:** the old Commons' history is exported and preserved (photos, milestones, the good years of #games) — memories are honored, then the rented hall is closed mindfully

## How you can help (no tech skills required)

- **Tell the truth about what hurts.** The most valuable contribution is honesty about what drains you and what you'd need
- **Join a working group** — Care, Design, Welcome, Build. All four need non-engineers most
- **Be a design reviewer** — especially if you're disabled, overwhelmed, or skeptical: you are the target user, in the best sense
- **Volunteer hours through the Commons Bank** — building the Commons counts, and your hours are honored like anyone's
