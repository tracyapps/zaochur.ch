# What We're Building

> **Status:** Draft v0.1 — for community review
> **The one-line version:** The Zao Commons is the church building, online — her rooms, her rhythms of rest and celebration, owned by the community and gentle to the exhausted.

Below is the full shape, described in plain language and mapped to roadmap phases (see the [Roadmap](06-roadmap.md)). Everything here is offered for discernment; the rooms are named, but nothing is frozen.

---

## The floor plan

### 🚪 The Front Door *(Phase 1)*
Where newcomers arrive and members orient. A link and a welcome — no Discord account, no app store.
- A warm join flow: who we are (the three pillars, in our words), what to expect, a house tour from a real human
- The day's gentle digest, right on the doorstep: what's happening today, this week
- Connection Form ties in, so "Get Connected" on our website means something here too
- Ways in that fit each person: quiet lurker-first steps, greeter conversations, Harbor discovery

### ⛪ The Sanctuary *(Phase 2, deepened in Phase 3)*
The sacred room: worship, prayer, reflection.
- Prayer wall with **careful visibility controls** — "community," "pastors only," "a few trusted people," anonymous — because a prayer request can out a person (SAFETY-5)
- Worship gatherings (Virtual Harbors) and service archives: sermons, music, liturgy
- Seasons of the church year reflected gently in the room's look — the building keeps time with us

### 🛋️ The Living Room *(Phase 2)*
General fellowship and events — the room where the community just... is.
- Conversations that feel like sitting with friends, not posting into a channel
- Events: what's happening, who's going, "want to go together?" — finding a companion for a march, a meal, a hard appointment
- Low-key presence: "a few folks are gathered" — the room is warm when people are in it

### 📚 The Library *(Phase 2)*
Books, study, and long thoughts.
- Reading groups and discussions that honor slow, considered replies (async-first)
- Shared shelves: what we're reading, recommendations, resources for the work of justice

### 🎮 The Rec Room *(Phase 2 for the daily ritual; more in Phase 4)*
Play — which for this community is daily and beloved. The games channel is among the most alive spaces we have, and its ritual deserves a *home*, not a channel.

**The daily ritual, kept and ennobled** (the pattern we already love from the #games channel):
- **A daily puzzle for the whole community** (starting with Wordle): one puzzle a day for everyone, in-app — no external accounts
- **Spoiler-safe sharing:** results post as colored grids, never letters — the fun of comparing without ruining anyone's day
- **The collective streak:** we keep *our* streak, together — it celebrates showing up for each other, and it never scolds (ETHICS-1). Miss a day? The community's streak absorbs it with grace; that's what a body does
- **The daily crowned digest:** a friendly end-of-day recap — the day's grid gallery, a crown 👑 for the day's best score, warm words for anyone who played. Score attaches to the game and the day, never to a person's worth (Principle 6)
- **Our own games, coming home:** two games the community already loves were built by one of us — ***doku*** (stardoku.app, a daily sudoku-style puzzle) and **draw-tionary** (draw-tionary.app, a drawing-and-guessing game), both currently living as Discord activities. Both will be ported into the Rec Room as native web games, sharing the same daily-ritual pattern and spoiler-safe result cards

**Games we'll grow into:** async drawing rounds (draw when you have energy, guess all day — low-pressure by design), simple tabletop and party games, community-made games. Games are how we play together across distance; they're treated as core community life, not a novelty drawer.

### 🎨 The Art Gallery *(Phase 4)*
Where we pin up our work and make things together.
- Walls that members can hang work on — visual art, writing, music, crafts — with artist's statements if desired
- Viewings and showings: scheduled moments to wander the gallery together and hear from the artists
- Collaborative pieces: community projects built in the open over weeks

### 🎵 The Music Studio *(Phase 4, honestly scoped)*
Music-making, with physics respected:
- **Layer-recording:** the "virtual round" — hear what's been recorded, add your track, pass it on. Jam sessions become built-together songs, which is its own beautiful thing
- Song shares, listening parties, and set lists for Harbors
- **Honest note:** real-time jamming over the internet is physically impossible (latency), so we won't pretend otherwise. Members who want to push the edge can be pointed to specialist low-latency tools (e.g., Jamulus) for special occasions — outside the Commons' promises

### 🏘️ The Neighborhood *(Phase 1–2)*
Mutual aid and material love.
- **The Commons Bank, built in from the start** (ported from ZAO Bank — see below)
- **The give-away shelf:** gifts, no money, no metrics — offer and claim with dignity
- Local coordination by region *and* interest — regions as context, never permission (a ZAO Bank principle kept)
- "Need a hand / have an hour" — the Commons Bank's front porch

### 🌅 The Harbors *(Phase 2–3)*
Rooms for each Zao Harbor and for Virtual Harbors.
- Each Harbor gets a hearth: announcements, discussion, worship materials, who's gathering this week
- Virtual Harbor rooms with voice/video (Phase 3) for gathered worship across distance

### 🤝 Affinity Spaces *(Phase 2)*
Groups with real walls.
- Affinity groups (by identity, interest, or care need) with **self-chosen membership, visibility, and guidelines** (INCLUSION-3)
- The platform enforces their boundaries without question and without admin visibility — a closed room is closed

### 🕯️ The Quiet Room *(Phase 3)*
A room for being together without words.
- Body doubling: sit together (cameras optional) and do the hard thing alongside others who are also doing the hard thing
- A shared focus timer, a candle that burns while you stay, and no obligation to speak
- Grief and heaviness space: for when the world is on fire and church needs to hold you quietly

### 💌 Post & Pathways (cross-cutting)
- **Digests, not firehoses:** the daily or weekly letter — everything gentle, nothing chasing
- Direct messages, disappearing on request, never admin-visible
- Export & delete: your words are yours (PRIVACY-3)

## The Commons Bank *(Phase 1 port, Phase 2 woven in)*

Our time bank — already designed, already loved — comes home into the Neighborhood. One hour of anyone's time equals an hour of anyone's time; nobody's hour is worth more than another's; and it runs on abundance and relationship, not scarcity, guilt, or shame.

Ported with its soul intact (from the ZAO Bank design document):
- Immutable exchange receipts; balances derived, never edited
- Regions as context, never permission
- Narrative reputation: public appreciations, private thank-yous, personal private notes (never admin-visible), self-authored profile tags
- The safety pattern that anchors this whole project: **flags reduce exposure immediately; humans determine outcomes**
- No leaderboards, no star ratings, no rankings — ever

The current WordPress implementation's logic ports cleanly; the plugin-shaped parts stay behind.

## What this is, all together

A front door that's actually open. A sanctuary that keeps our secrets. A living room with friends in it. A bank where time is love. A gallery, a studio, a library, a rec room, a quiet room. **A church building — wherever we are.**
