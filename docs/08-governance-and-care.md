# Governance & Care

> **Status:** Draft v0.1 — for community review
> **Inherited from:** the ZAO Bank safety design, discerned by this community
> **The throughline:** *Safety without carceality. Accountability with grace.*

A building is not cared for by its walls but by its keepers. This document describes how the Commons is tended — who does what, how harm is handled, and how power is kept accountable.

## Roles

| Role | Who | What they carry |
|---|---|---|
| **Members** | Everyone who calls the Commons home | Full participation; the right to raise anything; the standing expectation of kindness |
| **Caretakers** | Trusted, trained, *rested* volunteers (the ZAO Bank "core team" pattern) | Flag review, safety response, guideline stewardship — with least privilege and audit trails |
| **Room Stewards** | One or two members per room (Sanctuary: pastors; Rec Room: game ritual keepers; etc.) | Their room's tone, rhythm, and moving day |
| **Harbor Leaders & Pastors** | Existing Zao leadership | Pastoral care escalation, worship life, the Sanctuary's trust |
| **Builders** | The Build working group | Code, infrastructure, documentation — accountable to the same care rules as everyone |

- **Least privilege:** no role can see everything. Private notes and DMs are visible to *no one* — including caretakers, including builders (a ZAO Bank rule, kept).
- **Two-person rules** for sensitive actions (un-hiding content, member restrictions).
- **Rest is structural:** caretaking terms are seasonal, with overlap, so no one becomes indispensable (ETHICS-4).

## When something goes wrong: the flag path

*Flags reduce exposure immediately; humans determine outcomes.*

1. **Anyone flags** anything — message, room post, profile, game result — with an optional note. There is always the option: **"I want this on record, but no action right now."**
2. **Automatic, immediate, reversible:** the content is *hidden from view* (not deleted). Jobs come off listings; messages leave inboxes. The reporter is told exactly what happened and what happens next. Nothing else is automatic — no bans, no strikes, no algorithms (SAFETY-1).
3. **Human review:** caretakers — two where possible — restore or keep hidden, contact parties privately as needed, and choose from care-first responses: a conversation, mediation, a cooldown, pastoral care, or (rarest) a temporary restriction.
4. **Appeal and repair:** every decision can be appealed to a different pair of caretakers; the goal is restored relationship, not removal. Carceral reflexes are named as such when they appear.
5. **Record and transparency:** decisions are logged (reporter protected); each season the community hears what happened and what changed — in aggregate and in plain language.

**Safeguards inherited from ZAO Bank:** flag rate-limiting (against weaponized reporting), no analytics over flags, no access to private notes, flag history visible only to caretakers, nothing ever auto-deleted.

## Community guidelines

- Existing Discord server norms are the starting text; Phase 0's Care working group rediscerns them *with* the community, in plain language, translated as needed (INCLUSION-5).
- Guidelines describe the kind of room we're keeping, not a penal code. When in doubt: *"Is this how we treat a guest at Christ's table?"*

## Power, honesty, and repentance

- **Caretakers and builders are also members under covenant.** Their mistakes get the same grace-and-accountability path as anyone's.
- **The Decision Log is public.** Significant choices, trade-offs, and their "why" — so power operates in daylight.
- **When the community overrules us, we write it down and say thank you.** That's not failure; that's the body working.

## The annual rhythm of care

Each season/year: safety review (with anonymized incidents), privacy review, accessibility audit, caretaker rotation & rest check, and one open community conversation: *what is this building doing to us, and for us?*
